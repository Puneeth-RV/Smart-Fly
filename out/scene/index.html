What the fly sees

The game reduced to the contrast retina the optic lobe is fed. Near track fills more of the field, as under real perspective.

The brain is unmistakably alive — spikes propagate the full five stages and the descending neurons pick a swipe every frame. It still plays badly, and measuring why was the point. The correct move is 59.7% decodable from the retina and 15.7% from the Kenyon cells: the signal is lost at roughly 25–30% per synapse. Activity is easy to mistake for function, and that test is the one missing from every other connectome-plays-a-game demo.
